Booking, Payment, Cancellation & Refund Policy

A simple and patient-friendly policy for website bookings.

Booking & Payment

Website bookings paid through JazzCash are treated as pending until Lahore Medical Lab verifies receipt of the payment. Patients should quote the generated Booking ID and send payment proof to the official Lahore Medical Lab WhatsApp number 0303-4470883.

Please verify the JazzCash recipient name and selected package/service before making payment. A payment screenshot or receipt is supporting proof; final confirmation is issued after LML verifies the received payment.

Rescheduling & Cancellation

Patients may request rescheduling before sample collection or testing begins. LML will make reasonable efforts to accommodate the requested date/time, subject to service and home-sampling availability.

If a booking is cancelled before sample collection, testing, or another booked service has begun, the paid amount is eligible for refund after payment verification.

When Service Has Started

If sample collection, testing, a home-sampling visit, or another part of the booked service has already been provided or incurred, any refund will be reviewed according to the portion of service already used and the actual circumstances.

Wrong or Duplicate Payment

Duplicate payments, wrong amounts, or payment against an incorrect package should be reported to LML with the Booking ID and transaction proof. After verification, LML will arrange the appropriate adjustment or refund.

If LML Cannot Provide the Service

If Lahore Medical Lab is unable to provide the booked service, the patient will be offered a suitable rescheduling option or an appropriate refund.

Home Sampling & Special Offers

Home sample collection is subject to location, timing and staff availability. Time-limited or day-specific offers, including Sunday-only packages, must be used according to the stated offer conditions; LML may offer rescheduling where operationally possible.

Need help? Contact Lahore Medical Lab on WhatsApp / Call 0303-4470883. Refund or adjustment requests are processed after verification of the booking, payment and service status.
