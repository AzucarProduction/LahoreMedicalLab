Our Detailed History

The journey of Lahore Medical Lab & Diagnostic Center.

Since 2015

Built Step by Step Around Accessible Diagnostics

Lahore Medical Lab & Diagnostic Center began in 2015 with a long-term vision to develop reliable, accessible diagnostic services. Its growth has progressed through laboratory development, regulatory compliance, new locations, collection-center expansion, preventive health programs and home sampling services.

2015

Foundation

Lahore Medical Lab & Diagnostic Center was established, beginning its journey in diagnostic laboratory services.

2016

PHC Registration & Raiwind Operations

PHC registration followed in 2016. The Raiwind location operated during the early development phase from 2016 to 2018.

2019–2024

Johar Town Phase

Lahore Medical Lab continued its operations from Johar Town, Lahore, while expanding diagnostic services and its referral and collection network.

2022

PCR Capability

The laboratory received PHC approval for COVID-19 PCR testing, strengthening its molecular diagnostic capability.

2023

ISO 9001

ISO 9001 certification marked another step in the laboratory's quality-management development.

June 2024

Township Head Office

The current Head Office and Main Laboratory became operational at Building No. 25 1C1, College Road, Near Lajnah Chowk, Township, Lahore.

2025–2026

Quality, Programs & Network Development

The laboratory continued strengthening MSDS compliance, quality systems, home sampling and healthcare programs. In a relatively short span of time, Lahore Medical Lab expanded its reach to a network of 35 collection centers across different cities and areas, improving access to diagnostic services for more communities.

Our Laboratory Today

Lahore Medical Lab's Head Office supports routine diagnostic work through Hematology, Biochemistry, Special Chemistry, Microscopy and PCR facilities. Routine biochemistry, hematology, serology and microscopy services are performed in-house, with additional testing supported through appropriate referral arrangements when required.

Beyond Routine Testing

The laboratory has developed preventive screening packages, the Madad Program, Healthy Family Program, workplace health initiatives, Parents Health Screening, home sample collection and other community-focused healthcare programs.

Our Direction

Our long-term direction is to continue developing Lahore Medical Lab from a diagnostic laboratory network into a broader, integrated diagnostic and medical services platform while maintaining accessibility, patient support and quality systems.
